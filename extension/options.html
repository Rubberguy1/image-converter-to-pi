🎵 Pixel Pusher Now Playing

This extension sends whatever is playing in your browser to your Pixel Pusher panel. It runs on this computer and talks to the Pi over your network — the Pi doesn't need to host anything for you.

Your Pi's address and port — the same one you open the web app at. Example: http://raspberrypi.local:8000 or http://192.168.1.50:8000.

In the panel's web app, set Music sync → Source to Browser and press Start syncing.
